Payment & Refund Policy

How card, crypto, reservations, refunds, reversals, and permanent marks interact.

VERSION 2026-08-25 · LAST UPDATED 25 AUGUST 2026

1. PRICE

The base price is USD $4.99 per mark. One checkout may include 1–1,000 marks. Applicable card taxes are calculated at Dodo Payments checkout based on the information and jurisdiction used by the provider. Crypto quotes, network conditions, and provider minimums may change the amount of cryptocurrency required to satisfy the USD-denominated order.

2. RESERVATION IS NOT PAYMENT

A browser selection or reservation does not create a permanent mark. Cells are temporarily reserved by the server and can expire. Payment starts only after a valid server reservation exists.

3. CARD PAYMENTS — DODO PAYMENTS

Card transactions are completed on Dodo Payments' checkout. Dodo Payments states that it acts as Merchant of Record for those purchases. Its Buyer Terms, tax handling, fraud controls, refund process, and privacy policy apply in addition to HUMANITY/1's policies.

Read Dodo Payments Buyer Terms

4. CRYPTO PAYMENTS — NOWPAYMENTS

Crypto payments use NOWPayments. You must send the exact supported currency/network to the generated address and comply with the displayed amount and timing instructions. Sending another token, using the wrong network, sending after expiration, or sending to an old address may be irreversible.

5. SERVER VERIFICATION CONTROLS THE MARK

A success page, browser redirect, screenshot, wallet screenshot, card authorization message, or client-side state is never sufficient to seal a mark. HUMANITY/1 seals only after the backend independently validates the payment state through the configured provider verification chain and the database integrity checks succeed.

6. FAILED, EXPIRED, OR CANCELLED PAYMENTS

If payment is not successfully verified, no new permanent mark is created. A failed or expired payment normally releases the reservation when it is safe to do so. An active provider payment may keep the cells reserved temporarily to prevent double payment or conflicting ownership.

7. DUPLICATE PAYMENT PROTECTION

The backend allows only one active payment channel for a reserved batch. Do not intentionally open or pay multiple provider invoices for the same reservation. If you believe you were charged twice, preserve the provider receipts and request review through the applicable payment support channel.

8. REFUNDS, DISPUTES, REVERSALS, AND CHARGEBACKS

Refund rights depend on applicable law and the payment provider's rules. For card transactions, Dodo Payments' Buyer Terms state that refunds are handled under its process and may be assessed case by case, without limiting rights that cannot legally be waived.

For crypto, refunds are not automatically guaranteed because blockchain transfers, network fees, conversion, and routing can be irreversible. Where a refund is legally required or otherwise approved, the operational method and amount may depend on the provider, available settlement route, fees, and applicable law.

Artwork integrity: if a mark has already been validly sealed, a later refund, dispute, reversal, or chargeback is recorded as a financial event and does not automatically erase, move, recolor, or transfer the historical artwork mark. Mandatory law overrides this rule where required.

9. PAYMENT VERIFIED BUT SEAL BLOCKED

If a provider confirms payment but an integrity check prevents a complete atomic seal, HUMANITY/1 will not silently create only part of the purchased batch. The payment is placed into a review path so the issue can be investigated and an appropriate remedy, retry, replacement process, or refund can be determined under provider rules and applicable law.

10. NO GUARANTEE OF RESALE OR FINANCIAL VALUE

Purchasing a mark is participation in a digital artwork. HUMANITY/1 does not operate a resale market and does not promise liquidity, appreciation, return, profit, or any investment value.
