Help with your account, uploads, videos, photos, rankings, safety, and other INDEX Video features.

Contact Support

Email: support.indexapp@gmail.com

Account & Sign-in

For sign-in problems, account access, or profile issues, contact support and include the device type and the app version you are using.

Videos & Photos

If an upload is delayed, fails, or does not appear correctly, send us a short description of what happened and your device model.

Safety & Reports

You can report content inside the app. For urgent account or safety concerns, contact our support email directly.

Delete Your Account

Open INDEX Video, go to Settings, and use the Delete account option. If you cannot access your account, contact support for assistance.

Privacy & Legal

Privacy, user agreement, community guidelines, copyright, and other legal information are available inside the app under Settings → Legal.

What to Include

  • Your device model and operating system
  • INDEX Video app version
  • A short description of the issue
  • A screenshot when helpful
